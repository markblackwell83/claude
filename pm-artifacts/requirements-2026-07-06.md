## Feature Opportunity

### Opportunity: Compliance-Ready Third-Party Integration Hub for Medical Affairs

**Why now:** The medical affairs AI market is fracturing around incompatible silos—Veeva CRM, IQVIA analytics, EHR platforms (Epic/Cerner), MMIT payer data, and patient voice tools (Semalytix). Enterprise pharma teams waste significant time manually reconciling data across systems, and 83% of pharma organizations face a documented AI compliance gap. Incumbents (Veeva, IQVIA) are building inward, not outward—leaving a wide-open opportunity for a neutral, compliance-first integration orchestration layer that connects these systems and surfaces synthesized intelligence where MSLs and clinical users actually work.

**Strategic fit for ADVANCE AI:** This directly serves the role's two domains—third-party integrations and MSL products. An integration hub creates compounding platform value: every new connector deepens the moat, and every connected system generates richer insights for the MSL product layer.

## User Stories

### Story 1: Pre-Built Connectors to Core Medical Affairs Systems

**As a** Medical Affairs Platform Administrator at a mid-size biopharma company,  
**I want** pre-built, compliance-validated connectors for Veeva Vault CRM, IQVIA Expert Engagement Platform, Epic EHR (FHIR R4), and MMIT Searchlight,  
**so that** I can integrate our core systems in days rather than months without involving the IT security team in a lengthy custom API build.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am authenticated as a Platform Administrator in ADVANCE AI,  
**When** I navigate to the Integrations Hub and select the Veeva Vault CRM connector,  
**Then** I should see a guided OAuth2/SAML configuration wizard, pre-populated field mappings for HCP profiles, interaction records, and medical inquiries, and a test connection validation that confirms bi-directional sync within 60 seconds.

**Given** a connector is active and syncing,  
**When** a record is created or updated in the source system (e.g., Veeva interaction logged by MSL),  
**Then** it should appear in ADVANCE AI within 5 minutes via near-real-time sync, with a full audit log entry recording the source system, field values, and sync timestamp to satisfy 21 CFR Part 11 traceability requirements.

**Given** a connector authentication token expires or the upstream API returns an error,  
**When** ADVANCE AI detects the failure,  
**Then** the Platform Administrator receives an in-app alert and email within 15 minutes, and no data is silently lost—failed sync events are queued for retry with exponential backoff for up to 24 hours.

### Story 2: MSL Pre-Call Intelligence Briefing

**As a** Medical Science Liaison preparing for an HCP interaction,  
**I want** an AI-generated, compliant pre-call briefing that synthesizes the HCP's recent publications, current formulary access for my product, open medical inquiries, and prior MSL interaction history,  
**so that** I can walk into every meeting with context that would otherwise take 30+ minutes to manually compile.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am an MSL with an upcoming interaction logged in my ADVANCE AI calendar or synced from Veeva,  
**When** I open the HCP profile within the ADVANCE AI mobile or web app,  
**Then** I should see an AI-generated briefing containing: (1) the HCP's last 3 relevant publications with one-sentence AI summaries, (2) current formulary tier and any recent payer policy changes (sourced from the MMIT connector), (3) open medical inquiries from my company's medical information system, and (4) a chronological summary of the last 3 MSL interactions with this HCP. Briefing should load in under 10 seconds.

**Given** the briefing is generated by AI,  
**When** I view the briefing,  
**Then** each AI-generated summary must display a "Source" citation linking to the original document, and a "Confidence" indicator (High/Medium/Low) so I can calibrate reliance on AI-generated content. Any content sourced from a regulated system (EHR, formulary) must include a "Last verified" timestamp.

**Given** I am working in a region with different data residency requirements (EU vs. US),  
**When** the briefing is assembled,  
**Then** patient-identifiable or HCP-identifiable data must only be processed in the jurisdiction-appropriate data zone, with GDPR-compliant consent flags honored before EU HCP data is surfaced.

### Story 3: Cross-System Scientific Insight Capture and Synthesis

**As a** Medical Affairs Insights Lead,  
**I want** MSL field observations (captured post-interaction in ADVANCE AI) to be automatically tagged, structured, and synthesized alongside patient voice signals and published evidence,  
**so that** I can identify emerging scientific questions and competitive intelligence patterns without manually reviewing hundreds of individual MSL reports.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** an MSL submits a post-call report in ADVANCE AI,  
**When** the report is saved,  
**Then** the AI must automatically extract and tag: (1) therapy area, (2) product referenced, (3) HCP sentiment (positive/neutral/negative/inquiry), (4) any adverse event signals (flagged for pharmacovigilance review), and (5) competitive product mentions. Tags must be editable by the MSL within 48 hours of submission.

**Given** a minimum of 10 tagged field reports are available for a product/indication,  
**When** the Insights Lead opens the Insight Synthesis dashboard,  
**Then** the system must surface a weekly AI-generated trends summary showing: top 3 emerging scientific questions by frequency, sentiment trend over the last 4 weeks, and any adverse event signal clusters requiring pharmacovigilance escalation. Each trend must link to the source reports.

**Given** an adverse event keyword or phrase is detected in a field report,  
**When** the tag is applied,  
**Then** the report must be automatically routed to the designated pharmacovigilance queue within the ADVANCE AI platform, and the MSL's manager and the safety team inbox must receive a notification within 1 hour. This workflow must be configurable per product and per regulatory geography.

### Story 4: Compliance-Validated AI Content Generation for MSL Materials

**As a** Medical Affairs Content Manager,  
**I want** AI-assisted generation of MSL leave-behind summaries, medical FAQ responses, and scientific exchange documents that are pre-checked against approved label content and off-label guardrails,  
**so that** I can reduce the time-to-field for compliant MSL materials without increasing regulatory risk.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** a Content Manager initiates a new MSL document in the AI Content Studio,  
**When** the AI generates a draft,  
**Then** the draft must be automatically cross-referenced against the current approved product label (sourced from the integrated regulatory document repository), with any claim that is not label-supported highlighted in red and blocked from export until resolved or approved by a designated Medical Reviewer.

**Given** a Content Manager submits a document for Medical Review,  
**When** the Medical Reviewer approves, requests changes, or rejects the document,  
**Then** the full review workflow—including version history, reviewer identity, timestamp, and approval status—must be stored in an immutable audit log that satisfies FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records requirements. The audit log must be exportable in CSV and PDF formats.

**Given** the final approved document is exported by an MSL for use in a field interaction,  
**When** the document reaches its configured expiration date (default 90 days, configurable),  
**Then** the document must be automatically archived in the system, the MSL must be notified that the material is expired, and re-use of the expired version must be blocked with a prompt to request an updated version.

### Story 5: Integration Health Monitoring and SLA Dashboard

**As a** Platform Administrator responsible for the ADVANCE AI integration layer,  
**I want** a real-time dashboard showing the health, sync status, error rate, and SLA compliance of all active integrations,  
**so that** I can proactively resolve issues before they impact MSL workflows and demonstrate integration reliability to compliance auditors.

#### Acceptance Criteria

**Given** I am logged in as a Platform Administrator,  
**When** I navigate to the Integration Health dashboard,  
**Then** I should see, for each active connector: (1) current status (Active/Degraded/Failed), (2) last successful sync timestamp, (3) records synced in the last 24 hours, (4) error count and error type breakdown for the last 7 days, and (5) SLA adherence rate (target: ≥99.5% uptime per connector per month). Dashboard must refresh every 5 minutes.

**Given** a connector's error rate exceeds 5% of sync attempts in any rolling 1-hour window,  
**When** the threshold is breached,  
**Then** the Platform Administrator and a configurable escalation contact must receive an automated alert via email and in-app notification within 10 minutes, with a direct link to the affected connector's error log showing the last 20 failed records and their error codes.

**Given** a compliance audit is in progress,  
**When** the auditor requests an integration activity report,  
**Then** the Platform Administrator must be able to export a complete, immutable log of all integration events (creates, updates, deletes, errors, retries) for any specified date range in PDF and CSV, with each record including: source system, record ID, field changed, old value, new value, timestamp, and user/system actor.

## One-Page Product Spec: Compliance-Ready Medical Affairs Integration Hub

### Problem Statement

Medical affairs teams operate across a fragmented technology stack: MSL interactions are logged in Veeva, KOL insights live in IQVIA, formulary data resides in MMIT, patient evidence is siloed in tools like Semalytix, and EHR data is locked behind Epic APIs. Synthesizing these sources into coherent, actionable intelligence for MSLs and Medical Affairs leaders requires manual effort that slows down insight cycles and creates compliance risk when data is assembled informally. Enterprise procurement teams increasingly require compliant, auditable AI workflows—but the dominant platforms either can't interoperate or charge prohibitive enterprise minimums that exclude the mid-market.

### Proposed Solution

Build a **Compliance-Ready Medical Affairs Integration Hub** within the ADVANCE AI platform that provides:

1. **Pre-built, compliance-validated connectors** for Veeva Vault CRM, IQVIA Expert Engagement, Epic FHIR R4, and MMIT Searchlight—deployable by Platform Admins with no custom engineering.
2. **Agentic MSL Intelligence** that assembles real-time pre-call briefings, post-call insight tagging, and AI-assisted compliant content generation—all from integrated data sources.
3. **Unified Insights Synthesis** that aggregates MSL field observations, patient voice signals, and published evidence into a structured intelligence layer for Medical Affairs leadership.
4. **Compliance Infrastructure** (21 CFR Part 11 audit logs, HIPAA data zoning, GDPR consent flags, pharmacovigilance escalation workflows) embedded as non-negotiable platform behavior, not optional configuration.
5. **Integration Health Dashboard** providing real-time observability, SLA tracking, and auditor-ready activity exports.

### Out of Scope (v1)

- EHR clinical decision support or patient-facing features
- Direct FHIR write-back to EHR systems (read-only EHR integration in v1)
- Custom connector builder (self-serve; only pre-built connectors in v1)

- Integration with non-pharma verticals (medtech, devices, diagnostics)
- Real-time adverse event regulatory submission (pharmacovigilance flagging only; submission via existing safety systems)
