## Executive Summary

The Advanced AI health tech market serving Medical Science Liaisons and medical affairs teams is entering a consolidation phase following three years of fragmented innovation. Agentic AI architectures are displacing point solutions, while the September 2025 expiry of the Veeva–Salesforce non-compete has reset the integration stack expectations of every pharma buyer. Real-world evidence (RWE) generation, patient-voice analytics, and compliance-first data governance are the decisive differentiators as the global pharmaceutical AI market tracks from ~$2.5 B (2026) toward $21.5 B by 2035 (27% CAGR). Clinical trial platform spend is independently projected to reach $4.5 B by 2029. Platforms that natively bridge MSL field intelligence with Veeva Vault CRM and EHR data streams hold a structural advantage in the current buying cycle, and that gap is not yet closed by any single vendor.

## Market Trends

### 1. Agentic AI Is Replacing Copilots in Medical Affairs
The market has shifted from AI-assisted search and summarization toward autonomous multi-step agents that plan, execute, and audit their own workflows. Semalytix, MMIT, Veeva, and Salesforce have all launched agent-layer products. Medical affairs teams now expect agents that can execute pre-call intelligence briefs, synthesize congress insights, and draft medical information responses with minimal human prompting — and full audit trails. *Implication for ADVANCE AI:* an agentic MSL workflow layer is no longer a differentiator; it is table stakes within 18 months.

### 2. The Veeva–Salesforce Split Resets Integration Expectations
The expiry of the non-compete in September 2025 created a bifurcated CRM market for the first time. Veeva holds ~80% of life sciences CRM seats globally and launched Vault CRM AI Agents (December 2025). Salesforce Life Sciences Cloud is winning 70+ customers and uses FHIR Connect for EHR interoperability. Pharma buyers now evaluate every point solution by how cleanly it integrates with whichever platform they choose. *Implication for ADVANCE AI:* third-party integration quality (Veeva Vault and Salesforce LSC) is a direct purchase criterion — not a nice-to-have.

### 3. Real-World Evidence (RWE) and Patient Voice Are Moving Into MSL Workflows
Venture investment in healthcare AI agents surged in 2025, with outsized interest in RWE and regulatory-intelligence applications. Platforms like Semalytix are indexing 33 M publications and embedding patient-social-listening into pharma strategy workflows. Regulators are releasing AI guidance frameworks in 2026–2027. MSLs are increasingly expected to bring RWE context to KOL conversations. *Implication for ADVANCE AI:* an RWE synthesis module within the MSL pre-call brief workflow is a near-term product opportunity.

### 4. AI Pathology and Biomarker Intelligence Are Entering Clinical Trial Conversations
Mindpeak's $15.3 M Series A and partnerships with Discovery Life Sciences and Leica Biosystems signal that AI biomarker quantification (Ki-67, HER2, PD-L1) is being pulled into clinical trial workflows. MSLs supporting oncology indications will increasingly need platform access to biomarker data from trial sites. *Implication for ADVANCE AI:* partnerships or API integrations with digital pathology vendors could differentiate the platform in oncology medical affairs.

### 5. Compliance Infrastructure Is Now a Competitive Moat
Regulatory frameworks for AI in medical affairs are tightening globally. GDPR-compliant architectures (Semalytix), CE-IVD certification (Mindpeak), and adverse-event detection built into RAG pipelines are now expected rather than exceptional. Buyers in regulated markets will eliminate vendors that cannot demonstrate full audit trails, human-in-the-loop controls, and separation from promotional workflows. *Implication for ADVANCE AI:* investing in compliance infrastructure early creates a moat that is expensive for startups to replicate.
